ImmersiveDialogue 2.0.2 fixes the walk animation in dialogue and adds an optional add-on.

**Fixed:** the walking animation in dialogue now follows what you press, not how fast the body happens to be moving. Two things that came from the old way are gone: flipping from A to D (or D to A) no longer stops Skif dead and restarts the walk, and the half-second of straight walk-start before a strafe from standstill no longer happens.

**New, optional:** the zip now also carries an add-on that hides the "press X to skip" prompt in dialogue. With free look, moving or looking around kept making it pop up. The skip key itself still works.

**Install:** drop `ImmersiveDialogue-v2.0.2.zip` into Vortex. Because the zip holds two paks, Vortex lists the files and asks which to install: tick the three `zzz_ImmersiveDialogue_20_P` files for the mod alone, or **Install All** for the mod plus the no-skip-prompt add-on. Deploy, replacing 2.0.1. Manual install: copy the three files from `Main` (and, if you want the add-on, the three from `Optional-NoSkipHint`) into `Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\`.

**Still known (cosmetic):** Skif's arms are not visible in dialogue except while he gestures. Being worked on.
