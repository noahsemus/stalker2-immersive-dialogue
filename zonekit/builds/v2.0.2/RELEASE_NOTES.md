ImmersiveDialogue 2.0.2 fixes the walk animation in dialogue and adds an optional add-on.

**Fixed:** the walking animation in dialogue now follows what you press, not how fast the body happens to be moving. Two things that came from the old way are gone: flipping from A to D (or D to A) no longer stops Skif dead and restarts the walk, and the half-second of straight walk-start before a strafe from standstill no longer happens.

**New, optional:** `ImmersiveDialogue-NoSkipHint-v2.0.2.zip` hides the "press X to skip" prompt in dialogue. With free look, moving or looking around kept making it pop up. The skip key itself still works. Install it alongside the main mod if you want it; leave it out if you don't.

**Install:** drop `ImmersiveDialogue-v2.0.2.zip` into Vortex and deploy, replacing 2.0.1, or copy the three `zzz_ImmersiveDialogue_20_P.*` files into `Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\`. Same for the add-on zip (its files are `zzz_ImmersiveDialogueNoSkipHint_20_P.*`).

**Still known (cosmetic):** Skif's arms are not visible in dialogue except while he gestures. Being worked on.
