ImmersiveDialogue 2.1.2: the real fix for footsteps that wouldn't stop.

**For players**
- **Footsteps no longer get stuck after a conversation.** If a conversation ended at the exact moment one of Skif's footsteps played (while walking around in it), that footstep kept repeating, even standing still, until you talked to someone again. 2.1.1 fixed a different suspect; this fixes the actual cause.
- Nothing else changes; the no-skip-prompt add-on is the same as before.

**Install:** drop `ImmersiveDialogue-v2.1.2.zip` into Vortex, replacing the previous version, and tick the six files from `Main` (or **Install All** to add the no-skip-prompt add-on). Manual install: copy the six files from `Main` (and optionally the three from `Optional-NoSkipHint`) into `Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\`, over the old ones.
