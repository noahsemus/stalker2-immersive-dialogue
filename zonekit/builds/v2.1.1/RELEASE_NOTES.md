ImmersiveDialogue 2.1.1: fix for footsteps that wouldn't stop.

**For players**
- **Footsteps no longer get stuck.** Rarely, after walking away from an NPC during a conversation, Skif's footsteps kept playing nonstop (standing still, even after the conversation ended) until you talked to someone again. The mod now clears its walking input as soon as no movement key is held, whatever ends the conversation.
- Nothing else changes; the no-skip-prompt add-on is the same as in 2.1.0.

**Install:** drop `ImmersiveDialogue-v2.1.1.zip` into Vortex, replacing the previous version, and tick the six files from `Main` (or **Install All** to add the no-skip-prompt add-on). Manual install: copy the six files from `Main` (and optionally the three from `Optional-NoSkipHint`) into `Stalker2\Content\Paks\~mods\`, over the old ones.
